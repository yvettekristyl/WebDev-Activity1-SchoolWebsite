DEPED CURRICULUMS

The DepEd Curriculum is the Philippines' K to 12 basic education framework designed to build essential life, career, and academic skills. Under its updated MATATAG revision, it focuses on streamlined subjects to master core literacy, numeracy, values, and national identity from Kindergarten through Grade 12.

Junior High School (JHS) — Grades 7 to 10

JHS transitions students from elementary education to deeper academic rigors, preparing them for specialized tracks in high school .

Special Curricular Programs (SCPs)

Special Curricular Programs cater to learners with high potential or special talents in specific disciplines. These programs run parallel to Junior High School, where students take the standard core subjects but swap out regular TLE electives for specialized, intensive training.

Senior High School (SHS) — Grades 11 and 12

Under the modern Strengthened SHS Curriculum, the previous 15 core subjects are optimized into 5 core foundational subjects to maximize student career and college readiness while lowering academic workload.
